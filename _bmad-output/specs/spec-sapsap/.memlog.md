- (capability) CAP-1: Contributor registration via phone OTP, profile management, and reputation score tracking.
- (capability) CAP-2: Mission discovery via list/map, GPS proximity filtering, and temporary mission reservation.
- (capability) CAP-3: On-site mission execution with forced in-app camera capture, GPS proximity check (<100m), and questionnaire completion.
- (capability) CAP-4: Contributor wallet management (pending/available/earned) and Mobile Money withdrawal requests.
- (capability) CAP-5: Business campaign creation wizard (type, location, questions, budget) and payment processing.
- (capability) CAP-6: Business campaign progression tracking on map/dashboard and CSV/Excel result export.
- (capability) CAP-7: SapSap admin moderation of campaigns, manual mission submission verification, KYC validation, and payout approval.
- (capability) CAP-8: MVP anti-fraud enforcement via server timestamp, photo hashing, Device ID tracking, and GPS verification.
- (constraint) Geographic restriction: Ouagadougou pilot market only for MVP.
- (constraint) Camera capture must occur in-app with strict gallery upload prohibition for submission photos.
- (constraint) GPS proximity check required before mission execution (default radius <= 100m).
- (constraint) Tech stack: Ionic/Capacitor Android app for contributors, Web apps for Business and Admin, Modular Monolith backend (NestJS/Laravel) + PostgreSQL.
- (constraint) Payments & Payouts integrated with Mobile Money (Orange Money, Moov Money).
- (constraint) Strict enforcement of prohibited mission categories (military sites, surveillance, private property intrusion).
- (assumption) Mobile Money APIs or manual payout workflows are operational for Burkina Faso region.
- (assumption) Android app coverage satisfies >90% of contributor device market in Ouagadougou.
